Processing & Booking FAQs

Frequently asked questions by local farmers and merchants

You can schedule a delivery slot using our online booking tool. Once confirmed, you can drop off your fresh crop directly at our unit in Mannackanad P.O., near Marangattupilly, Kottayam. We also coordinate pickup trucks for batches larger than 500kg.

Since JME operates large-capacity commercial crop dryers, the minimum batch weight we accept is 50 Kilograms. This allows us to maintain efficient energy usage and keep costs low for everyone.

To promote rural entrepreneurship and agricultural value addition, the government provides financial subsidies under the Prime Minister's Employment Generation Programme (PMEGP). We pass a 10% discount on drying services directly back to small farm owners to encourage local preservation.

Traditional sun-drying exposes crops to dust, insects, bird droppings, and damp weather, leading to fungus (molds) and nutrition degradation. JME's controlled closed-chamber dryers use dust-filtered hot air to dehydrate produce quickly, preserving natural color, vitamins, flavor, and ensuring 100% hygiene.
