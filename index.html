The Youth Entrepreneurship Story

Josemon Jacob & JME Dryer Unit

Founded in 2020 by then-17-year-old student Josemon Jacob during the peak of COVID-19 lockdowns. Struck by the plight of local farmers in Kottayam who were forced to watch their crops decay due to transportation disruptions, Josemon converted a small backyard farm dryer into a local rescue unit.

With government support under the PMEGP loan scheme, JME expanded to multiple industrial hot-air drying chambers processing over 1.5 to 2 tonnes of crops per batch, saving crops from decay, producing chemical-free products, and generating local employment.
